## Summary

Renewals stores your information on your own device. It does not require an account and does not send your data to the developer or to any server.

## What the app stores

The information you enter, such as item titles, categories, expiry dates, reminder settings, costs, notes and optional photos of documents or receipts. This information stays on your device in the app's private storage.

## What the app does not do

- It does not create accounts or ask you to sign in.
- It does not collect, transmit or sell your personal data.
- It does not contain advertising.
- It does not use analytics or tracking tools. [CONFIRM before publishing]
- It does not ask for passport, license or ID numbers. Please avoid typing such numbers into the notes field.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is used |
|---|---|
| Notifications | To show reminders before your items expire. You can deny this, but reminders will not appear. |
| Camera and photos (only when you choose to add an attachment) | To attach a photo of a document or receipt to an item. Photos are stored inside the app and are not added to your gallery. |

## Backups and sharing

If you use the export feature, the app creates a file and opens your phone's share sheet. Where that file goes is decided by you. The developer does not receive it. Backup files do not include attachment photos.

## Data deletion

You can delete any item inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored by the app on your device. Exported backup files that you saved elsewhere are under your control and must be deleted by you.

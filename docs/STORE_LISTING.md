## Full description: English

Forgetting an expiry date can cost you money or cause real trouble. Renewals keeps every date in one place and reminds you before it is too late.

**Track everything that expires**
• Passport, visa, driving license, vehicle papers
• Insurance policies
• Subscriptions and their monthly and yearly cost
• Product warranties
• Domain and hosting renewals

**Simple reminders**
• Choose when to be reminded, for example 90, 30, 7 and 1 days before
• See what is overdue, due this week and due this month at a glance
• Mark an item as renewed and keep its history

**Private by design**
• Works fully offline
• No account, no sign-up
• Your data stays on your phone
• Backup and restore with a file you control

**Made for everyone**
• Bangla and English
• Light and dark theme
• Clean, fast and lightweight

Add a photo of a document or receipt to any item so it is always at hand.

Note: reminders depend on your phone's notification and battery settings. The app includes a short guide if reminders do not arrive.
